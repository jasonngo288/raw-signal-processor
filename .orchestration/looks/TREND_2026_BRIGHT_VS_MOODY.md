## 1. Verdict (my own survey, before the 42-source report)

Neither pole wins. Best-supported: warm, soft, natural film (gentle contrast, slightly lifted black, creamy highlights, muted-not-flat greens/blues, warm skin, fine-to-medium grain) plus a separate "imperfection" current (flash/digicam/CCD/grain). Heavy dark-moody is described as softening into "moody natural"; bright/clean survives as true-to-life (Apple Bright style), not as a film look. Confidence M for "no single winner", L for any numeric split. No primary 2026 grading data from Instagram, TikTok, Lemon8, Xiaohongshu, VSCO, Adobe or Pinterest exists in what I could reach.

## 4. Critique of the report's method (based on what the coordinator told me)
- Ranking items that rest on sources the report itself flags as unverified (Pellica +118 %, Accio Shopee counts) should carry zero weight in a ranking; if they feed the 40/20/10/10/20 split, the split is not evidence-based. Same for InspireFusion (not found) and Instagram Flash dated 5/2026 (earlier in reality; and single-source).
- Evidence type mismatch: Envato +31 % is stock video templates over 4 weeks; Fuji X Weekly is page views among enthusiast Fujifilm owners (skews to Western photographers using X-series cameras, not phone users; M); VSCO Film 02 is a product release; Mastin is seller positioning. None measures what phone users apply. Counting them as "votes" is a category error.
- Fuji recipe names are trademark stock names (Portra, Kodachrome, Gold). Fine as evidence of taste, not as preset names (the repo already flagged "Fuji Soft 400"). The preset list in the report must be re-checked for names before anything ships.
- Vietnam evidence = Tet only: seasonal (vivid/warm red-gold peaks around Tet), so it says "vivid wins in Jan-Feb", not an annual trend. The vivid share (20 %) is therefore biased upward unless the user's launch month is Tet.
- No numbers on the look itself (black level, saturation, etc.): the 10-preset split cannot be turned into tone/colour values without section 5 of the earlier note (now section 6 here).
- The 40/20/10/10/20 split is a design allocation, not a measured demand; I found no source that quantifies it. Treat as a hypothesis for a blind user test.

## 6. Colour characteristics (colourist translation; proposals, L, from earlier vendor descriptions)
- Warm natural film: black 0.02-0.035, contrast 0.95-1.06, shoulder 0.84-0.9, cream highlight tint (+0.002..+0.006), sat 0.9-1.0, olive greens (-5..-10 deg, chroma 0.75-0.9), skin peach with hue held, grain 0.2-0.4. (goldenDay, softPortrait, everydayGreen cover it.)
- Soft moody natural: black 0.015-0.025 with shadow detail, contrast 1.05-1.12, warm browns, muted greens 0.75-0.8, no teal, mean Y about -4 % vs base.
- Airy/pastel portrait: black 0.03-0.045 (cap raise of p1 about 0.03 over base), contrast 0.82-0.9, pivot 0.5-0.55, skin chroma kept, mint greens, cyan pastel skies. (softPortrait, pastelCool.)
- Digicam flash: black 0-0.005, contrast 1.15-1.3, clipped near skin/whites, casts uncorrected, cool-green or magenta shadow tint, chroma noise, vignette 0.25-0.35. (flashDigicam.)
- Disposable/CCD: saturated + grainy (disposable); bluish-clean, hazy, lifted black, cool highlights (CCD). Date stamp and light leak are overlays, not LUT.
- Slide-vivid: black 0.004-0.01, contrast 1.15-1.3, dense reds, cyan-leaning blues. (vividFine.)
- Warm vintage B&W: contrast 0.9-1.0, black 0.03-0.05, cream/brown highlight tint, grain 0.3-0.5 (monoSoft has no tint).

## 8. Does the 2026-11-10 tune (5 looks 3-6 % darker/denser) fit?
Trend evidence neither confirms nor contradicts -3..-6 %. Dense Kodachrome/Gold-style looks match the Fuji list (H list, M relevance); warm-faded leading (per the report) argues AGAINST darker for goldenDay/everydayGreen if "faded" means lifted blacks and lighter mids: the tune lowered black p1 slightly (goldenDay 0.0227 to 0.0209, everydayGreen unchanged) and mean Y. Recommendation: keep density in reds/blues only, hold mean Y within about -2 % on everyday looks, test with a blind A/B (10-20 target users, indoor/cafe/night files). raw-bench can measure meanY/black p1/clip/chroma/skin hue; it cannot say what users like.
