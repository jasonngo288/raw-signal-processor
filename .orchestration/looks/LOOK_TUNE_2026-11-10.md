## Look-function additions (FilmLooks.swift only; defaults keep old output)
- `HueBand.lightness` — relative OKLab L change inside a band (the per-colour luminance slider every reference uses),
  faded in by chroma (full at C ≥ 0.05) so greys never move.
- `LookSpec.density` — subtractive dye density: L × (1 − density·min(C/0.2, 1)). Makes strong reds deep instead of
  neon (skill: color-science §1, film-emulation stage 2; MoodRoll fail criterion "neon reds").
- Landmark comment fixed: ColorChecker skins sit at OKLCh h ≈ 41° (computed: dark 42.3°, light 40.7°), not 55°.
