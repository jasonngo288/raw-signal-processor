## Method
- Each RR stock names one of the 11 colour looks; there is no new LUT. The 11 looks are 7 listed (no flashDigicam)
  plus 4 trials.
- What each stock sets:
  - its own intensity;
  - grain from its ISO: 100 (0.18 / 0.050), 160 (0.22 / 0.055), 200 (0.28 / 0.060), 400 (0.38 / 0.070),
    800 (0.50 / 0.080), 800T (0.55 / 0.085);
  - the base look's halation and vignette.
- R5 is checked by test: two stocks on the same look differ in ISO, or by ≥ 0.2 intensity at the same ISO.
- The scene rules (dark / high ISO / people −, landscape +) still add on top, per frame.

## Weak spots (honest)
- **Shared looks.** goldenDay carries 3 stocks, vividFine 3, softPortrait 3, brightTravel 3. Vàng/Ấm 200 and
  Rực Rỡ/Lá Phong 100 differ only by intensity (the brief's own R5 example allows "ấm rực vs ấm dịu").
- **Lá Phong 100** needs its own look: reds + cyan and a light leak, and the finisher has no leak effect.
- **Chuẩn 160/400/800** are close to base: ΔE ≈ 0.9–1.2 on IMG_1901 / mooncake at 800 px. That is "lab standard"
  as asked, but users may see little difference.
- **Gió 400** sits on a low-confidence trial look (trialCoolFade).
- **Sao Mai / Sao Hôm** are on everydayGreen, whose emerald greens may be too "cool" for a warm everyday stock.
- The brief's open points are still open: the "*" mark in the price list, YesStar's legal check (R3a), the date of
  the price list, the meaning of "RR", and the Chân Dung 400 tier.
- Not yet run: roll-consistency bench for the 21 stocks, and the review page with RR names.
